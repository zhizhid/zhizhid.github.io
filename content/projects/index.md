## Med-BERT

A clinical foundation model for structured clinical data. Med-BERT applies BERT-style pretraining to electronic health records, enabling transfer learning for clinical prediction tasks.

## gene2vec

A distributed representation embedding model for genes based on their co-expression patterns. Similar to word2vec for natural language, gene2vec learns vector representations of genes that capture biological relationships.

{{< github-stars repo="jingcheng-du/Gene2vec" >}}

[GitHub](https://github.com/jingcheng-du/Gene2vec) | [Paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12864-018-5370-x)

## PBWT-based Algorithms

Advanced Positional Burrows-Wheeler Transform (PBWT) data structures and algorithms for population genetics informatics, enabling efficient haplotype matching and identity-by-descent detection at biobank scale.
