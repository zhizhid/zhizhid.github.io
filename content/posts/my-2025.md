Still shocked when this year of 2025 just whooshing by. One thing I feel blessed is AGI has not yet taken over the world. 

Every semester in the past 8 years I ask my students at the beginning of my Deep Learning class: "When do you think AGI will come?" 8 years ago hardly anyone would believe it would come; now most students have a timeline almost aligned with the AI luminaries. I hear the footsteps of the AGI.

Ever since I signed the "AI pause letter", I console myself by believing that at least my job of developing medical AI is clearly pro-human. Therefore I am not pure evil. 

Still I was worried about human losing control. While there are many opinions about this topic. One new realization I had this year is that current LLM does not yet have a persistent memory. Even some reports LLM refused to be turned off, it is probably just simulating a typical human emotion. But this threshold of persistent memory might soon be crossed.

"Human with AI will replace human without AI!" - shouted Jensen. Quickly I am pulled back to the reality filled with my own survival instinct. 

Only towards the end of the year did I start to catch up some of the crazy new AIs. Spending one day vibe coding with Claude Code with Opus 4.5 feels so surreal. I know I still have a long way to catch up such as Skills and MCPs, but I did shipped two projects that I have been wanting to do for a long time:

- [**PBWT tutorial**](https://github.com/zhizhid/PBWT-tutorial): A rudimentary PBWT tutorial with animation for the contruction.
- [**My personal website**](https://zhizhid.github.io)

Everyone is saying 2026 will be even wilder than 2025. Let's see.
